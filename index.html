Operating point of view

Six practices for production AI.

  1. 01Start with the decision

    Define the workflow change, user, baseline, and consequence before selecting a model.

  2. 02Make authority explicit

    Record what the system may recommend, decide, or do, together with prohibited actions.

  3. 03Require inspectable evidence

    Use provenance, evaluation, and abstention to support important outputs.

  4. 04Design the degraded mode

    Monitoring, fallback, rollback, recovery, and ownership belong in the initial architecture.

  5. 05Build the feedback route

    Give users a clear way to challenge results and improve the capability over time.

  6. 06Scale against evidence

    Expand when value, trust, operability, and organizational readiness are visible.
