Operating point of view

Six practices I use when designing Enterprise AI.

  1. Define the outcome first.State the decision or workflow change and its baseline before choosing a model.
  2. Set authority limits.Document what the system may decide, recommend, or do, together with prohibited actions.
  3. Require inspectable evidence.Use provenance, evaluation, and abstention to support each important result.
  4. Design for operation.Monitoring, degraded mode, rollback, and ownership belong in the initial architecture.
  5. Plan how people will use it.Provide a clear route for users to learn, challenge, and improve the capability.
  6. Scale against evidence.Expand when value, trust, operability, and organizational readiness are visible.
