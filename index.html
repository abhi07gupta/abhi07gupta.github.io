Semantic intelligence

Structured context makes AI easier to reason about and govern.

Current architecture work explores reusable semantic and knowledge foundations for heterogeneous enterprise information at a public-safe level of abstraction.

Architecture lesson

When enterprise meaning is explicit — entities, relationships, provenance and constraints — reasoning can become more reusable, inspectable and resilient to change.
