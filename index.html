Operating point of view

Six principles for enterprise AI that earns the right to scale.

  1. Outcome before mechanism.State the decision or workflow change and its baseline before choosing a model.
  2. Boundaries before autonomy.Define what a system may decide, recommend, or do—and what it must never do.
  3. Evidence before confidence.Make provenance, evaluation, and abstention stronger than fluent output.
  4. Operations are architecture.Monitoring, degraded mode, rollback, and ownership belong in the initial design.
  5. Adoption is a system property.Design how people learn, challenge, and improve the capability.
  6. Scale is earned.Expand only when value, trust, operability, and organizational readiness are all visible.
